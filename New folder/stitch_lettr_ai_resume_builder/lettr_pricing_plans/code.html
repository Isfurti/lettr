Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our career experts.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Our Pro subscription is billed monthly or annually and can be cancelled at any time through your dashboard. You will retain access to your premium features until the end of your current billing period.

How does the ATS scoring work?

Our proprietary algorithm analyzes your resume against 50+ common Applicant Tracking Systems used by Fortune 500 companies. It identifies missing keywords, formatting issues, and structural errors that might cause your resume to be rejected automatically.

What is "AI Bullet Rewriting"?

It's our flagship feature. We use GPT-4 tuned on successful executive resumes to turn passive descriptions ("Responsible for sales") into high-impact, data-driven achievements ("Architected a $2M revenue growth strategy").

Is there a student discount?

Absolutely. We offer a 50% discount for students and recent graduates within 12 months of graduation. Simply contact our support team with a valid student email address.
